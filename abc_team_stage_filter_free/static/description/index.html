ABC Team Stage Filter (Free)

A small team selector next to the Kanban view-switcher icons that scopes the board — records and stage columns — to one sales team at a time. Built for the CRM Pipeline, works with Helpdesk and any other team + stage combo out of the box.

🧩 Two teams, one merged mess

As soon as a company has two or more sales teams, each with its own funnel, the shared Pipeline Kanban shows every team's stages mixed into one board — a Sales team sees Purchasing's stages, Support sees Renewals', and nobody's funnel reads clean anymore.

Odoo's own "show every stage always" behavior for Kanban columns is by design (so a stage never silently disappears) — it just means nothing narrows the board for you automatically. This control does.

👥 One board, two audiences

Administrators and managers overseeing every team still want the full merged view — nothing changes for them unless they ask: leave the selector on "All teams" and the board looks exactly like it always has.

Salespeople and team leads who only work one funnel want the opposite: pick their own team from the same selector, and the board narrows to just their stages and their records — no scrolling past columns that aren't theirs, no cards from a team they don't manage. Same control, same click, whichever way someone needs to see their day.

How it works

  • The selector shows up automatically once a user belongs to 2 or more teams for the model — for everyone else, nothing changes at all.
  • Picking a team filters the Kanban's records to that team and hides every stage column that isn't the team's own (or shared across teams) — computed from the team relation on the stage model itself, not guessed.
  • The choice is remembered per user, per database, so it's still set the next time that person opens the Pipeline.

Reusable by design

The control is config-driven, not hardcoded to CRM. Out of the box it covers the Sales Team / Stage pair on crm.lead; registering another model with the same team + stage shape (e.g. Helpdesk teams and ticket stages) is one line of JavaScript, no core changes: 

import { registerTeamStageModel } from "@abc_team_stage_filter_free/team_stage_filter";

registerTeamStageModel("helpdesk.ticket", {
    teamField: "team_id",
    stageField: "stage_id",
    teamModel: "helpdesk.team",
    memberField: "member_ids",
});

Zero setup, zero risk

  • No configuration screen, no new menu, no business models — pure frontend.
  • Installing it changes nothing until a user actually belongs to 2+ teams. Uninstalling it removes the selector cleanly.
  • Free and open source (LGPL-3), part of the ABC controls line.
